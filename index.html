Since August 2022...

Some people walk into our lives unexpectedly... and somehow become family.

To Eskimo :D

I met somebody in August 2022... probably 2–3 days after his birthday because I didn't know about it. A crazy, introverted, sensitive, innocent boy who was unaware of the world but indeed intelligent enough. A well-behaved person, yet he always ran away whenever we met in person, even though we chatted late into the night, mostly talking about him.

It was new to me... Guess what? Before meeting him, I had only known one person other than him, someone who had been with me since childhood. But this one started as a random friendship that I never expected would become such a close brother-sister bond.

Yes, we gave each other nicknames... funny ones too like dog breeds... yet still hesitated to face each other. We had our exams scheduled on the same floor. I still remember intentionally giving stationery to his class crush because he was a bit late to the exam. I even tried to make the first move and waited for him after the exam, but I forgot he was so good at studies that he had keep writing until the very last minute. There I was, standing outside waiting impatiently and in the end... we greeted each other and escaped again.

Time flew and a lot happened. He became good at communicating and why wouldn't he? when I was the teacher. He fell for one of the prettiest girls, and I fell for my first-ever childhood best friend. We were different, of course, because we knew our basic boundaries and everything went well until some manipulators made it worse. Those fake people convinced him that I had said bad things about him, which I would never do because he was one of the very few people who truly meant a lot to me.

I still find it funny how he drank four bottles of alcohol and one of his new female friends randomly asked me to save him. But that incident actually scared the shit out of me. I cried in school in front of him twice because I kept getting flashbacks of his innocent face and all the efforts we had made to help him come out of his introverted shell. It was the first time I found myself brave enough to stand up for someone I truly trusted because there was nobody for me in that traumatizing school except fake friends.

Honestly, he was a great friend, but he sucked at relationships. Maybe he never communicated with her the way we communicated as friends. I'm really bad at remembering things, but I remember almost everything about him because he was such a different creature... and he still is indeed but I guess time changes everyone.

We had a lot of misunderstandings that might still be somewhere within us but they never affected our bond much. But since I'm over with his cousin, I feel like it's affecting me again... that feeling of slowly losing somebody. Something I should probably worry about, because we really were a great brother-sister duo. Oh, I just remembered Raksha Bandhan is on the way. And guess what...? I can rely on this one brother because he actually knows how to protect me and I'm so proud of him for how much he's grown and how understanding he has become.

He left so unexpectedly, but I guess it was better this way. Otherwise, I would've made it even harder by crying my heart out. And why wouldn't I? His beloved first love had already left me here... and now him too? I kinda miss him now, knowing that I won't be able to see him for a really long time. But it's okay, because he's building a great life ahead, and that too all by himself. :)
